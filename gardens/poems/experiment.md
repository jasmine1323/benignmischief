title: Experiment
tags:
  - poem
---

The cure to the pain  
was to know how to put it into language,  
once you could translate the suffering  
into insight it burned less.

Because I was the world's greatest psychiatrist  
and had long ago initiated an experiment operating in the real world.

I would give subconscious cues to people to behave in certain ways  
and treat me in certain ways:  
this treatment would dictate my behavior  
and I'd prove once and for all that behavior is  
circumstantially induced. Further, by encouraging  
people to abuse me I was gaining experiential  
knowledge of the world: a first hand account  
of how things felt on the interior and my unique  
power of introspection  
would translate those experiences  
into general knowledge for the masses,  
once the experiment was done.
